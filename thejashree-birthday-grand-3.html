The Most Important Thing

Your Family · Your Roots

Before the wishes, the dreams, the big plans — there is one thing that will always matter most.

Thejashree — you are turning 20, and the world ahead of you is wide and full of possibility. But before you race into it, pause here for just a moment.

Think about the people who woke up every single day — not for themselves, but for you. Your parents. The ones who stayed up worrying about things they never told you about, who quietly carried burdens so your path could feel a little smoother. Who celebrated your smallest wins like they were the greatest things in the world.

They did not do it for a thank you. They did it because to them, you are everything.

As you grow into this new chapter — please keep them close. Call them when something good happens. Call them when something hard happens too. Let them be part of the life you are building, not just observers watching from a distance.

There will come a day when you would give anything just to hear their voice or sit beside them. Do not wait for that day. Give them that today.

Take care of them — the way they have always, quietly, taken care of you. 🌸
